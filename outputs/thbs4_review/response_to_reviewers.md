# Response to reviewers

We thank all three reviewers for their detailed and constructive critiques. The manuscript has been substantially revised. Below we address each major and minor comment in turn, indicating the specific changes made.

---

## Reviewer 1 — muscle & myokine biology

**Comment 1 (§9, receptor paragraph: CD47 is an over-claim).** Agreed. The CD47 candidacy is not supported by the cited work and is incompatible with subgroup-B thrombospondin biology, which lacks the type-1 repeats required for CD47 engagement. **Change made:** CD47 has been removed from the candidate-receptor list. Integrin α2 and α2δ-1 are now retained as the only biochemically defined candidates from the cited literature, and a sentence has been added explicitly acknowledging that no unambiguously validated THBS4 receptor has been identified. CD36 is mentioned only as a speculative, non-validated candidate with appropriate hedging.

**Comment 2 (§9, ref. 4 mischaracterisation).** Agreed — ref. 4 (Lawler 1993) characterised THBS4 as a gene highly expressed in heart and skeletal muscle; it did not claim essentiality for skeletal development. **Change made:** the offending sentence has been rewritten to reference the early developmental focus on THBS4 in muscle and osteogenic contexts [ref. 4; ref. 58], and the critique of the field is now framed as a redundancy/compensation point rather than a contradiction with ref. 53.

**Comment 3 (§7, incorrect citation grouping for the AAA claim).** Agreed — ref. 26 and ref. 35 do not support the AAA conclusion. **Change made:** the sentence has been split. ref. 26 is cited only for TGF-β1/SMAD3-driven endothelial induction; ref. 35 is cited only for integrin α2/α2δ-1-mediated pro-angiogenic activity; ref. 43 alone is cited for the AAA knockdown finding.

**Comment 4 (§6, P387 mislabelled as a "pro-angiogenic variant").** Agreed — ref. 36 establishes P387 as a macrophage-adhesion-promoting variant, not as more pro-angiogenic than A387. **Change made:** the sentence has been reworded to "The P387 variant more actively supports macrophage adhesion and accumulation within atherosclerotic lesions than the common A387 variant [ref. 36]…".

**Comment 5 (§6, unsupported lesion-size qualifier).** Agreed — the "without altering overall lesion size" claim was not anchored to a specific result. **Change made:** the qualifier has been removed; the statement now reports only what ref. 36 directly establishes (P387 vs A387 comparison of macrophage accumulation).

**Comment 6 (§3, sweeping secretomics-criteria statement).** Agreed — the original wording implied no secretomics criteria had been applied to THBS4 anywhere, whereas cardiomyocyte work has established ER–Golgi transit and stimulus-coupled secretion. **Change made:** the statement is now cell-type-qualified: "the field has not yet applied these criteria to THBS4 in skeletal myofibres in the way that has been done for cardiomyocytes…".

**Comment 7 (§2, over-extension of ref. 17 in the Brody dissection).** Agreed — the mdx-worsening result and the secretion-defective mutant dissection are distinct observations. **Change made:** the two are now described in separate sentences, with the Brody dissection (WT vs secretion-defective on UPR and sarcolemma) treated on its own, followed by a discrete clause reporting the mdx result.

**Comment 8 (§1, tacit endorsement of circulating-concentration threshold).** Agreed — the criteria list is presented as field consensus without acknowledging that several of these benchmarks are themselves contested or post-dated by currently accepted myokines. **Change made:** a disclaimer has been added noting that the listed criteria are not universally applied and that several accepted myokines pre-date some of these benchmarks.

**Minor comments 1–10.**
- (1) Nomenclature has been standardised: **THBS4** for the human gene/protein, **Thbs4** for mouse; **TSP-4** is reserved for direct quotation of the older tendon/equine literature (Hauser 1995, Södersten 2006).
- (2) "Matricryptic factor" is now glossed on first use.
- (3) "TGF-β" has been standardised throughout.
- (4) ref. 13 (Benner 2013, SVZ astrogenesis via THBS4/Notch) is now cited in §5 as an additional paracrine-neural precedent.
- (5) §4 now contains an explicit statement that TGF-β1-driven THBS4 induction has not been formally demonstrated in skeletal myofibres.
- (6) The ref. 9 cohort description has been softened to "previously sedentary humans" pending verification of the original methods.
- (7) The "opposing inputs" citation in §7 has been narrowed to ref. 32 and ref. 31.
- (8) CD36 is now mentioned as a speculative candidate in §9; CD47 has been removed (see Major Comment 1).
- (9) The Section 1 LPL/VLDL claim is now qualified with "intra-muscular" on first mention.
- (10) A summary table has been added (new Table 1) mapping each criterion of the myokine framework (regulated transcription, secretion evidence, circulating detection, receptor engagement, in vivo phenotype) to the supporting references and the muscle-specific gaps.

## Reviewer 2 — ageing, metabolism & translation

**Comment 1 (§10, attribution of human age-related circulating-THBS4 decline to ref. 12).** Agreed — ref. 12 is explicitly a mouse study and has been incorrectly grouped with human data. **Change made:** ref. 12 has been removed from the human claim. The sentence now reads: "In mice, circulating THBS4 declines with age [ref. 12]; whether the same holds in humans is unknown." The same fix has been applied to the parallel misattribution in §9.

**Comment 2 (§5, "mechanistically distinct line of evidence" overstates ref. 12).** Agreed — ref. 12 is a single young-vs-old mouse proteomic screen with no compartment-resolved source attribution. **Change made:** the wording has been softened to "suggests", and the paragraph now explicitly states that the muscle-derived origin of the serum THBS4 deficit in old mice has not been demonstrated and that no equivalent human proteomic comparison stratified by age has been published.

**Comment 3 (§6/§7, dissociation of cardiac and large-vessel phenotypes).** Agreed — the cardiac hypertrophy/fibrosis phenotype [ref. 42] and the aortic dissection/aneurysm phenotype [ref. 41] are mechanistically distinct and arise in different vascular compartments. **Change made:** when the Ang II data are first introduced in §6, the compartment-specific framing is now flagged explicitly. The §7 synthesis now treats the two phenotypes as dissociable rather than as parts of a single "cardioprotective" narrative.

**Comment 4 (§5, methodological details on the de Jong cohort and effect-size anchoring).** Agreed — the matching on BMI and Fried frailty index could not be verified from the abstract and the central biomarker claim required quantitative anchoring. **Change made:** the matching claim has been qualified as the authors' reading of the methods pending verification against the full text, and the available effect-size, p-value, sample size and AUC data (as reported in ref. 14) are now stated explicitly.

**Comment 5 (§2/§3, conflation of "intracrine", "matricellular" and "matricrine").** Agreed — the three terms are used inconsistently. **Change made:** a single definitional paragraph has been added in §2 distinguishing intracrine (ER-resident action within the producing cell), matricellular/autocrine/paracrine (matrix-localised action), and endocrine (distant target organs). "Matricrine" has been removed.

**Comment 6 (§1, microvascular/oxidative-fibre localisation attributed to ref. 5/ref. 6).** Agreed — ref. 6's abstract does not establish microvascular localisation. **Change made:** ref. 6 has been removed from the microvascular claim and is now cited only for the ECM/LPL/VLDL/soleus-mass/grip phenotype; ref. 5 is retained for NMJ/interstitial localisation. (See also Reviewer 3 Major Comment 1.)

**Comment 7 (§4, section heading overstates the muscle-cell evidence).** Agreed — the SMAD3/SMAD2, HIF-1α and ATF6 axes have essentially no direct support in skeletal myofibres. **Change made:** §4 now opens with an explicit framing that the discussion is a mechanistic extrapolation from non-muscle cell types, with skeletal-muscle-confirmed mechanisms limited to the ATF6α–BiP–THBS4 axis.

**Comment 8 (§9, overstatement of ref. 4).** Agreed — ref. 4 does not claim essentiality. **Change made:** the sentence has been reworded to note that the high skeletal-muscle expression reported in the original identification [ref. 4] coexists with a lack of overt skeletal or bone-mass phenotype in null mice [ref. 53], suggesting redundancy or compensation.

**Comment 9 (§5, speculative prose presented declaratively).** Agreed — the "chronic mechanical and oxidative burden" sentence is reasonable speculation but was written as if documented. **Change made:** the speculative reasoning is now explicitly flagged ("we speculate that…"); declarative language has been removed.

**Comment 10 (LPL/VLDL phenotype framed as a "metabolic-myokine" claim).** Agreed — the LPL/VLDL observation is an intra-muscular phenotype and should not be lumped into the systemic-metabolism argument. **Change made:** the conclusion has been tightened to make explicit that the LPL/VLDL phenotype is an intra-muscular metabolic role and, if anything, cuts against a circulating-hormone model by pointing to local biology.

**Minor comments 1–15.**
- (1) "TurboID" is now capitalised on first use.
- (2) "the Brody programme" is now glossed on first use as "the body of work from the Brody laboratory".
- (3) The "candidate biomarker of early frailty" phrasing in §5 is now tied explicitly to the pre-frailty/frailty framework of ref. 14.
- (4) §6 now includes a brief note that no one has yet tested whether pregabalin/gabapalin modulates THBS4 signalling.
- (5) §7 now contains a one-sentence caveat distinguishing the replication case-control study [ref. 39] from the original association [ref. 38].
- (6) "the Brody programme demonstrates" has been replaced with neutral phrasing in §8.
- (7) §9 now explicitly prefers HSA-CreERT2 (or analogous inducible lines) for the proposed knockout experiments and notes that constitutive ACTA1-Cre/MCK-Cre will delete during development.
- (8) References with 2026 dates [ref. 28; ref. 29; ref. 55; ref. 43; ref. 1; ref. 59; ref. 3] have been verified and are now cited with article numbers/DOIs where possible.
- (9) §4 now notes that THBS1 harbours a CD47-binding domain absent from THBS4, which may explain divergent signalling.
- (10) §10 now includes a one-sentence statement of the minimal experiment that would tip the balance toward "established myokine" status.
- (11) §1 now cites primary demonstrations (Steenberg 2000 / Pedersen 2003) alongside review citations for the endocrine-muscle claim.
- (12) The working myokine definition from §3 is recapitulated in §10.
- (13) §6 now includes a short mechanistic comment proposing that P387 may gain an integrin-binding function absent from A387, whereas germline deletion removes both.
- (14) The §9 phrasing on circulating THBS4 now reads "[ref. 14; ref. 11] in humans; [ref. 12] in mice."
- (15) The manuscript now uses "candidate myokine" or "putative myokine" consistently when describing THBS4 in muscle.
